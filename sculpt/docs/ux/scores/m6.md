## Not verifiable from screenshots

- Hotkeys in general: F2, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+G, Ctrl+E, S, Shift+L, Shift+H, Ctrl+M, Tab switcher, and whether any of them work while hovering the viewport.
- Inline rename typing flow (only the open edit field is visible).
- Modifier clicks on thumbs: Alt+click to view the mask, Shift+click to disable the mask, Ctrl+click popover.
- Ctrl+drag duplicate, spring-open folders, auto-scroll during drag, multi-row drag order.
- Strength change by drag (the 40% value is visible, but not how it was set).
- Tooltips on thumbnails and icons.
- Live thumbnail refresh within about 1 s.
- Recursive Alt-expand.
- Whether a stroke on a locked layer is really blocked (only the chip text is visible).
- Copy/paste mask result and the flatten result.
- AA contrast ratios (needs measurement).
