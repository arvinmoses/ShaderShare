## Not verifiable from screenshots

- Hotkeys and whether they work while hovering the viewport: S, Shift+L, Shift+H, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+G, Ctrl+E, F2, Ctrl+L, Ctrl+Shift+N, Alt+M, Esc, Tab, and any strength scrub.
- Modifier clicks on thumbnails: Alt+click to view the mask, Shift+click to disable it, Ctrl+click for a popover.
- Tooltips, including the modifier hints on thumbnails.
- Whether Ctrl+drag duplicates, and how a dropped row is named ("Pores copy").
- Spring-open folders, auto-scroll during a drag, recursive Alt-expand, and multi-row drag order.
- Inline rename typing flow: commit with Enter, cancel with Esc. Only the open edit field is shown.
- Whether thumbnails refresh live, within about 1 s of a stroke.
- Strength set by dragging. Only the end state (40%) is shown.
- Whether a new layer is inserted above the selection and inside the current folder.
- What happens after Paste mask (that the ops are identical) and after Flatten (a new layer is made and the source is hidden).
- Contrast ratios (AA). Judged only by eye.
- The pointer path and the 400 px radius for the §9.7 tasks.
